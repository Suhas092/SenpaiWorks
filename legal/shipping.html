Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how SenpaiWorks handles the fulfillment and electronic delivery of digital 3D assets, as well as the transit, courier dispatch, and delivery of physical studio merchandise.

1. Overview & Product Types

SenpaiWorks provides both digital creative content (3D character rigs, models, animation templates) and physical studio merchandise (apparel, posters, art books, and physical collectibles). The shipping process depends on the type of product purchased.

2. Digital Assets & Instant Delivery

All digital products purchased on SenpaiWorks are delivered electronically with no physical dispatch or shipping fees:

  • Delivery Timeline: Instant upon successful payment authorization and verification.
  • Access Location: Download links and license keys appear immediately on the order confirmation screen, are stored in your Account Orders Tab, and are emailed to your registered address.
  • Shipping Fee: ₹0 (Free electronic delivery worldwide).
If you encounter any download interruptions or corrupted archive files, please reach out to our team within 48 hours at senpaiworks.official@gmail.com for immediate re-issuance.

3. Physical Merchandise Processing & Dispatch

For physical merchandise orders placed through the SenpaiWorks Store:

  • Order Processing Time: Orders are verified, quality-checked, and packaged within 1 to 3 business days (excluding Sundays and national holidays).
  • Courier Partners: We partner with trusted courier networks across India (including BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post Speed Post).
  • Tracking Number: As soon as your parcel is dispatched from our fulfillment center, an automated shipping confirmation with real-time tracking details is sent to your registered email and SMS/WhatsApp.

4. Estimated Delivery Timelines

Estimated transit times from the date of dispatch:

  • Metro Cities (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 3 to 5 business days.
  • Rest of India (Tier-2, Tier-3 & Regional Districts): 5 to 7 business days.
  • Remote or North-East Areas: 7 to 10 business days depending on local postal accessibility.

Please note that delivery times are estimates provided by carrier partners and may occasionally be influenced by severe weather, regional lockdowns, or peak festival seasons.

5. Shipping Fees & Transparent Checkout

Shipping charges are calculated based on package weight and destination PIN code. All applicable delivery fees are shown transparently at checkout before you authorize payment:

  • Standard Shipping: Flat delivery rate calculated during checkout based on delivery location.
  • Free Shipping Promotions: Orders eligible for special store promotions or exceeding specified cart thresholds receive complimentary free standard shipping.

6. Incorrect Address & Undelivered Packages

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping information (including street address, landmarks, PIN code, and active contact phone number). If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address or non-availability of the recipient across multiple delivery attempts, re-shipping charges may apply.

7. Contact Support

For tracking inquiries, dispatch status, or assistance with an ongoing shipment, please visit our Support Center or reach out directly at senpaiworks.official@gmail.com with your Order ID.
