Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how SenpaiWorks collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase 3D models, or order merchandise.

1. Introduction

SenpaiWorks respects your privacy and is dedicated to securing your personal information. This notice outlines what information we gather, how we use it, and the choices available to you regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by you during account creation, order checkout, or support communications:

  • Account Details: Name, username, email address, password hash, and user preferences.
  • Order and Shipping Details: Recipient name, delivery address, phone number, and purchase history.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, and local storage data for cart persistence.

3. Payment Security

Payments on SenpaiWorks are handled through encrypted, industry-standard payment gateways (including Stripe and Razorpay). We do not store or process raw debit or credit card numbers on our servers. All transactions comply with PCI-DSS standards.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize cookies and browser storage strictly to maintain active user sessions, retain shopping cart items, and save wishlist items. You can manage or disable cookie preferences directly in your browser settings.

5. Google reCAPTCHA

We use Google reCAPTCHA on our forms to mitigate automated spam and abuse. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Your Rights & Data Management

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can edit your details in your Account Center or contact us directly to request full account removal.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Notice, please visit our Support Center or reach out to our team at senpaiworks.official@gmail.com.
