Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Thank you for supporting SenpaiWorks. This policy explains our cancellation guidelines, exchange criteria, and refund timelines for digital asset licenses and physical merchandise.

1. Digital Asset Downloads (3D Models, Rigs & Projects)

Because digital assets (including Blender files, character meshes, texture maps, and animation project files) are intangible goods delivered instantly upon purchase:

  • Non-Refundable: All digital sales are final and non-refundable once the file download link has been accessed or downloaded from your account dashboard.
  • Corrupted or Defective Files: If an archive file is corrupted, missing textures, or technically inaccessible, please contact us within 48 hours of purchase. Our studio team will investigate and issue a verified replacement link immediately.
  • Incompatibility: Please check software version requirements (e.g. Blender 4.x+) listed on the product detail page before purchasing. Refunds cannot be issued for third-party software incompatibilities.

2. Physical Merchandise Cancellations

For physical goods purchased from the SenpaiWorks Store (apparel, posters, and studio merchandise):

  • Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You can cancel an order at any time before it has been marked as Dispatched. To request a cancellation, go to your Account Orders Tab or email us immediately with your Order ID.
  • Post-Dispatch: Once a package has been dispatched and handed over to courier partners, cancellations cannot be processed. You may, however, request an exchange or return upon receiving the item under our 7-day policy below.

3. Returns & Exchanges (Physical Merchandise)

We take great pride in our merchandise quality. You are eligible for a return or replacement under the following terms:

  • 7-Day Window: Return or exchange requests must be initiated within 7 days of verified parcel delivery.
  • Eligible Reasons: Items received damaged, torn, defective in print/stitching, or incorrect in size/variant compared to your order confirmation.
  • Condition Requirements: Items must be returned unworn, unwashed, in their original state with tags and studio packaging intact.
To initiate a return or replacement, email senpaiworks.official@gmail.com with clear photos or an unboxing video showing the defect, along with your Order ID.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Upon receipt and inspection of the returned merchandise (or verified cancellation prior to dispatch):

  • Approval Notification: We will notify you via email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Credit Timeline: Once approved, the refund is initiated automatically through our payment gateway (Razorpay). Funds will be credited back to your original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking) within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Bank Delays: If you have not received your approved refund after 7 business days, please check with your financial institution or UPI provider, as processing times vary by bank.

5. Shipping Costs on Returns

If the return is due to a defective, damaged, or incorrect item dispatched by SenpaiWorks, we will cover the return courier shipping costs. For voluntary size exchanges, customers may be responsible for the nominal return courier charge.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Cancellation and Refund Policy, please visit our Support Center or reach out to our team at senpaiworks.official@gmail.com.
