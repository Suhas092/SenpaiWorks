ABOUT

SENPAIWORKS

SenpaiWorks is a one-man journey exploring all styles of 2D and 3D arts and animation. In the future, it aims to research new ways to fuse technology with art and grow into a space for creative minds to build together.

The Man Behind SenpaiWorks

Hi, I’m the creator of SenpaiWorks — a B.Tech graduate from PES University, Bengaluru, India. I work alone on everything you see under SenpaiWorks. From 2D animation to 3D modeling and editing, I learn each part by myself using the internet and hands-on practice. I love turning my imagination into 2D art, then pushing it into 3D, and making it even more creative using the latest tools and technology.

Every project is built from scratch — no shortcuts, no team. Just passion, learning, and the drive to create something new. SenpaiWorks is where I turn ideas into real, visual stories.

How It Started

I’ve loved cartoons since childhood and always admired how artists bring simple drawings to life. As I grew up, I started sketching, moved into digital art, and began posting on Instagram. With a tech background, I wanted to bring my ideas to bigger screens — inspired by creators like Oda and Kishimoto.


After watching the Deadpool and Wolverine movie, I got hooked on the “Bye Bye” song and decided to make my own animation. I started in 2D, then moved to 3D, and mixed both styles. It was tough — I had to borrow a friend’s laptop since my PC couldn’t handle 3D. After six months, I finished it. Seeing how studios showed their logos before videos, I wanted to do the same — and that’s how SenpaiWorks began.

Why the Name “SenpaiWorks”?

The name SenpaiWorks comes from my long-time love for anime. Since childhood, I was fascinated by the way characters would call out “Senpai” in a cute, playful way — that word stuck with me and I grew attached to it.

When I first created an Instagram page, I started with the name Senpai, then changed it to Sketches by Senpai as I began posting drawings. Later, I shifted it to Artworks by Senpai to cover the variety of styles I was exploring.

But my creative journey actually began with coding, before I moved into 2D and 3D animation, digital art, and other media. Since I was working on both technical and artistic projects, I wanted a name that tied everything together. That’s when I decided on SenpaiWorks — a name that represents all the creations I build, from code to art.

The Road Ahead

SenpaiWorks aims to become a hub for hybrid creators — blending animation, AI, and engineering into bold experiments.

The platform is envisioned as a space where future artworks, animations, and technologies can be shared, explored, and learned through this website. While SenpaiWorks is currently driven by a single creator, the goal is to grow into a team of passionate individuals working together.

The vision is for creative minds to join in, contribute their skills, and build something meaningful with the help of new AI tools and innovations. Beyond art, SenpaiWorks also looks toward research and development, pushing ideas further until they become creations to be truly proud of.
