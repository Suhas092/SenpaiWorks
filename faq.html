What is SenpaiWorks?

SenpaiWorks is a creative project showcase and digital storefront run entirely by a single creator. It represents an ongoing creative journey exploring 2D digital art, charcoal drawing, 3D modeling, character rigging, VFX, and software engineering. It is the place where artistic expression meets programming and technology.

Who runs SenpaiWorks?

Hi! I am Suhas, a B.Tech graduate from PES University in Bengaluru, India. I am the sole creator, artist, and developer behind everything you see on this site. From drawing and 3D modeling to designing and coding this web platform, I build everything from scratch.

Why the name "SenpaiWorks"?

The name is inspired by my childhood love for anime. "Senpai" is an honorific used in Japanese culture to refer to a senior mentor or guide. I initially started my art page on Instagram under Sketches by Senpai and later Artworks by Senpai.

Since my interests span both creative animation and technical software engineering, I wanted a single name that represents all of my technical and artistic projects. "SenpaiWorks" ties them all together into one cohesive portfolio.

Is SenpaiWorks an official animation studio?

No, SenpaiWorks is not an official company or large animation studio. It is a personal one-man portfolio run entirely by Suhas. Everything from coding the site to drawing, 3D modeling, and visual composition is a solo self-taught project.

What software and tools do you use?

I select software depending on the project style:

  • 2D Digital Art & Graphic Design: Adobe Photoshop and Clip Studio Paint.
  • 3D Character Models, Rigging, & VFX: Blender.
  • Video Editing & Composition: Adobe Premiere Pro.
  • Traditional Art: Charcoal pencils, graphite, and toned paper.

How long does it take to complete an animation?

Because I handle every single step of the pipeline myself—including concept modeling, character rigging, lighting, animation, rendering, and compositing—timelines vary significantly.

A short loop or stylized character concept may take a week or two. In contrast, complex projects (like the Deadpool and Wolverine Mixed-Media Animation) can take up to 6 months of dedicated learning and rendering.

Can I download or use the art for my personal wallpapers or profile pictures?

Yes, absolutely! You are free to download and use the illustrations and character renders for personal wallpapers, device backgrounds, and social media profile icons. However, commercial use, modification, redistribution, or selling them is not allowed. If you share them online, a credit/tag to @senpaiworks_ on Instagram is highly appreciated!

Can I use the store assets in my own animations?

Yes, absolutely! The 3D character rigs, brushes, and blender shaders available in the Store are specifically designed for fellow animators and creators to explore, practice, and build their portfolios. Be sure to check the specific licensing info on each product page before starting commercial projects.

Do you accept custom commissions?

Yes! I take a limited number of custom commissions for 3D modeling, rigging, and character artwork. If you have a project or collaborative idea, feel free to reach out via our Feedback Page or email me directly at suhassenpai@gmail.com.

Are there any free resources or tutorials available here?

Yes! I love giving back to the community. You can find free project files, visual breakdowns, and workflow guides in our Learn Section. I also periodically share free 3D models, presets, and shaders on our community Discord server. Be sure to check the description links under each artwork page!
