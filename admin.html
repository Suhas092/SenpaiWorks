Trash & Recovery

Items deleted across SenpaiWorks are preserved in a 14-day grace period before permanent R2 purge.

14-Day Dual-Engine Safety: Deleted artworks, news articles, hero slides, and showcase items are soft-deleted in the database and safely quarantined in trash/ on Cloudflare R2. Restoring an item immediately resurrects the database row and moves the image back to its live directory. After 14 days, the automated purge engine permanently cleans up expired records.
