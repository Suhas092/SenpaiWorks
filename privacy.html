Data Protection & Privacy

SenpaiWorks Privacy Notice

Learn how we collect, protect, and manage your personal data when purchasing 3D assets, anime prints, and digital art.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to SenpaiWorks™. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, store, and process your information when you browse our website, create an account, purchase digital 3D models, or order physical merchandise.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, place an order, or contact customer support:

  • Account Data: Username, email address, password hashes, and profile preferences.
  • Order & Shipping Data: Full name, shipping/billing address, phone number, and order history for fulfillment.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and browser local storage parameters used for shopping cart persistence.

3. Payment Processing & Security

SenpaiWorks processes online payments securely through encrypted payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, UPI). We never store or record full credit/debit card details on our servers. All transaction processing is compliant with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS).

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential browser cookies and local storage items to provide core website functionality, including maintaining your active session, persisting items in your shopping cart, and saving wishlist preferences.

5. Google reCAPTCHA Protection

To prevent automated spam attacks and protect user accounts, our authentication and contact forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA. Your use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service.

reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and application data, to analyze user interactions and prevent abuse.

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can update your profile information in your Account Center or contact support to request account deletion.

7. Contacting Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Notice or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please visit our Support Page or reach out to our team at support@senpaiworks.com.
