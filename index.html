Why SenpaiWorks?

SenpaiWorks is a creative platform where 2D and 3D art, animation, and digital storytelling come together. It showcases a wide range of work — from frame-by-frame animations and 3D modeling to realistic portraits, logo design, and hybrid projects — all crafted with imagination, technical knowledge, and attention to detail. Beyond presenting finished works, it reflects a vision of exploring new ways to fuse art and technology, while inspiring and sharing creative ideas with others.

Hybrid Animation

Seamlessly blending 2D and 3D techniques to create visually striking and dynamic sequences.

Unique Artistic Vision

From 2D character designs and frame-by-frame animations to 3D modeling, realistic portraits, and logo design, each piece reflects a wide-ranging and creative skill set.

Innovative Storytelling

Animations and visuals are designed to engage and surprise, pushing creative boundaries.

Broad Technical Skills

Proficient across essential tools like Blender, ZBrush, After Effects, Photoshop, and more, enabling diverse and polished creative work.
